The Problem: India's Forgotten Water Tanks

Millions of homes, schools, hospitals, and apartment buildings store water in tanks. The water comes in clean — but sits for days or weeks. During that time:

What Happens Inside the Tank

  • Chlorine (the disinfectant) disappears in 1–2 days
  • Dirt and sludge settle at the bottom
  • Green biofilm grows on walls and floor
  • Bacteria and algae multiply

What You Experience

  • Water smells or tastes bad — but it's too late
  • Family gets stomach infections
  • You waste water draining the whole tank to clean
  • Cleaning is guesswork — every 6 months? Once a year?
WHO says: "The safe water chain breaks at the point of storage unless tanks are kept clean and covered." Today, nobody knows when to clean — until the water goes bad.

TankGuard's Solution: Guesswork → Evidence

A small solar-powered device sits on your tank lid. Sensors hang in the water and measure 5 things that actually change while water sits:

What It MeasuresWhy It Matters (Simple Terms)
Turbidity (cloudiness)First sign of sludge & biofilm — rises before you can see it
pH (acid/alkaline balance)Drift means algae, decay, or pipe corrosion
TDS (dissolved solids)Minerals leaching from settled dirt and scale
TemperatureWarmer water = faster bacteria growth
Water LevelHow long since last refill (water age)

These 5 numbers feed TankScore — one simple score that answers:

"Is it time to clean this tank?"

Answer: ✅ GOOD · ⚠️ WATCH · 🔴 CLEAN NOW

What Changes for You
  • Before: Clean every 6 months (waste effort) or wait for bad smell (too late)
  • After: Clean exactly when needed — get an alert on your phone
  • Before: Climb the tank, open the lid, guess
  • After: Open phone → see TankScore → tap "Flush Sludge" → done
  • No WiFi? No internet? No problem. Works completely offline. Your data never leaves your home.
